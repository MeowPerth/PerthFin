PerthFin is an unofficial, native Android client for Jellyfin, based on the open-source Findroid project.

Browse your movies and TV shows and play them directly from your Jellyfin server, with a fast native interface, picture-in-picture, chapters, trickplay previews and media segment skipping.

PerthFin is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Jellyfin project or the Findroid author.

You need your own Jellyfin server (version 12 or newer) to use this app.
